PRODUCT FAQS

A little more clarity.

Get to know Skin COAT before making it part of your routine.

Skin COAT is your skin’s everyday sunshine companion. It is a gel-based, non-greasy, fragrance-free sunscreen for every season. Wear it on its own or under makeup, and make sun protection an easy step in your routine.

Dr. Barira Shah and Dr. Shahid Manzoor developed Skin COAT. They are the founders of ZSB and co-run Glow Creations Clinic. The product idea was originally proposed by Professor Zahida Fazil.

Skin COAT’s primer-like formula is designed to provide sun protection and a smooth base for makeup. Depending on your skin and makeup routine, it may help you skip a separate primer.

Yes, Skin COAT is described as colourless and odourless.

Yes, Skin COAT is described as fragrance-free and non-greasy.

Skin COAT is labelled as suitable for all skin types, though individual responses may vary. Review the ingredients and seek personalised advice if you have sensitive, damaged or recently treated skin.

The product page lists the ingredients. Always refer to your physical product pack for directions and cautions.

Consistent sunscreen use helps protect skin from sun exposure that can worsen dark spots and uneven skin tone. Skin COAT can be part of a daily sun-protection routine; it should not be considered a standalone pigmentation treatment.

Product information is provided as general guidance. Follow the directions and cautions printed on your product packaging.
