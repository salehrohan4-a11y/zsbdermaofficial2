THE GLOW CREATIONS CLINIC CONNECTION

Created together.
With purpose.

Skin COAT began with an idea proposed by Professor Zahida Fazil. Her vision laid the foundation for the product, which was eventually brought to life and launched by the doctors behind Skin COAT.

ZSB is the skincare brand co-founded by Dr. Barira Shah and Dr. Shahid Manzoor. Both founders work in skin care and aesthetics, run their own aesthetic clinic, Glow Creations Clinic, and developed Skin COAT together.

Their first product is a sunscreen built around a gel-based formula, a smooth texture and primer-like qualities.

Skin COAT was initially supplied through doctors and is used by plastic surgeons in professional skin-care settings. It is also supplied to burn-care centres. With ZSB’s online presence, Skin COAT is now available directly to a wider audience.
